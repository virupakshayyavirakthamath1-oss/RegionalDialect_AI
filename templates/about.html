BCA PROJECT

Regional Dialect Grammar & Slang Checker

This project explores NLP for regional and code-mixed language, where standard English grammar tools often miss informal patterns.

How the system works

1. Input text→2. Dialect detection→3. Grammar + slang analysis→4. Correction + score

Technology

PythonFlaskSQLiteHTML/CSS/JSNLPReportLab

Research extension

The baseline is intentionally lightweight so it runs reliably on a student laptop. For your final research phase, you can replace or extend the rule engine with a fine-tuned Transformer trained on your custom corpus.

Suggested dataset fields
text · dialect · grammar_error · corrected_text · slang_word · slang_meaning · formal_equivalent
